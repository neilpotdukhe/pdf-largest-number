# Convergence — Final Decision

**Goal:** converge on 1–2 submission-ready YC applications (Summer 2026 RFS),
pressure-tested by adversarial YC-partner mock interviews.

## The funnel
- **15** RFS categories → cut the hard-tech ones a modal technical founder can't fund
- **6** software-buildable, RFS-blessed categories → drafted **5** specific companies
- **5** → adversarial panel (3 partner personas) → cut FLUX (0/3) & CORTEX; repivoted
  HARBOR → WARDEN (two reviewers independently invented the same company)
- **3** finalists (DENIAL/OVERTURN, MERIDIAN, WARDEN) → 2 mock interviews (skeptic +
  operator) → **2** finalists
- **2** → final per-company YC interview simulation → revised applications

## The two we converged on

### ★ PRIMARY — WARDEN (Software for Agents)
Runtime control plane + audit-log-of-record for AI agents in production.
- **Why it won:** the ONLY idea where founder-market fit is *provable*, not borrowed —
  a strong technical founder is the buyer, builder, and user. No domain gate, real
  budget forming (SOC 2 forcing function), OSS-led motion, first paid pilot ~month 2–3.
  Unanimous top pick across all 5 agents.
- **Its risk (year-2, underwritable):** "a feature an incumbent bolts on." Retired by
  owning the cross-tool compliance system-of-record fast.
- **To make it a YES:** 2–3 prod design partners, ≥1 paid pilot, and one concrete
  *auditor-forced-us-to-buy* anecdote (SOC 2 CC6.x finding closed by WARDEN's log).

### ◇ CONDITIONAL #2 — OVERTURN (AI-Native Service Companies)
AI-native behavioral-health insurance-denials recovery firm, contingency-paid.
- **Why it's #2, not #1:** bigger, more painful market — but three structural drags
  WARDEN lacks (PHI/BAA gating, negative working capital, services-margin skeptic tax)
  and an FMF field that's blank without a recruited RCM operator.
- **It flips to #1 IF:** the founder lands a design-partner book with a measured ≥60%
  automation rate AND commits an RCM operator (advisor-with-equity floor, cofounder
  ideal). Then the market size makes it the bigger prize.

## The one that got away
**MERIDIAN** (AI-native AMS challenging Applied Epic/Vertafore) — best *business model*
of all five, clearest enemy, best "why now." Cut because it's uninvestable-by-this-team
without an insurance-native cofounder our founder profile doesn't have. **This is your
#1 the day you bring that cofounder.**

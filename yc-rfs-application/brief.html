02

The two finalists

WARDEN

★ Primary
RFS · Software for Agents

The runtime control plane + audit-log-of-record companies install before they let AI agents touch production.

Fund · with traction
  • Why it won: the only idea where founder-market fit is provable, not borrowed — the founder is buyer, builder, and user. Unanimous top pick across all 7 partner sims.
  • Wedge: agents holding live prod creds with no leash = a SOC 2 nightmare today. OSS-led, first paid pilot ~month 2–3.
  • Moat: the cross-tool, tamper-evident action-of-record — identity vendors capture access granted, not actions taken.
  • To be a YES: 2–3 prod design partners, ≥1 paid pilot, and one real auditor-forced-us-to-buy anecdote.

OVERTURN

◇ Conditional #2
RFS · AI-Native Service Companies

An AI-native behavioral-health insurance-denials recovery firm — paid only when the clinic gets paid.

Fund · if the gate clears
  • Bigger market, higher gate. $260B in denied claims; contingency = the easiest first sale in healthcare.
  • The drags WARDEN lacks: PHI/BAA gating, negative working capital, a services-margin skeptic tax.
  • North Star: automation rate — the one number that separates a software company from a Manila BPO.
  • Flips to #1 IF: a design-partner book at ≥60% measured automation and a committed RCM operator/cofounder.

04

The one that got away

MERIDIAN

An AI-native agency-management system challenging Applied Epic & Vertafore — the hated, sticky, 20-year incumbents 40,000 independent insurance agencies run on. The panel's best-business verdict: clearest enemy, best "why now," expanding ACV, software margins. It was cut for one reason only — it mandates an insurance-native cofounder this founder profile doesn't have, and its "land on top of the incumbent" wedge means reverse-engineering a hostile closed system. Bring the cofounder and this becomes the biggest prize on the board.

05

What to run this week

Both tests resolve with phone calls, not code — the fastest way to kill or confirm each finalist.

WARDEN

Ship the OSS proxy + audit log (Show HN). Email 15 Heads of Platform/Security at companies known to run internal agents in prod: "Are your agents holding live prod creds right now — would you pay for scoped creds + a full audit log before your next SOC 2?"

GO ≥3/15 say "I'd pay this quarter" or real OSS installs.
KILL <3 and a silent launch → pain is 12 months early.
OVERTURN

Get de-identified denials from one friendly clinic; run the agent on 200 real cases for a measured automation rate. Email 15 practice managers + 5 roll-up VPs of RCM for a contingency book.

GO a book loads and automation ≥60%.
KILL 3 weeks, no book handed over → the access wall is fatal.
Recommendation

Build WARDEN now. Run the OVERTURN experiment in parallel. If the book and the RCM cofounder both materialize within ~2 weeks, submit both — otherwise submit WARDEN and keep MERIDIAN in your back pocket for the day the right cofounder walks in.
